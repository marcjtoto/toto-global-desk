**OFFICIAL CLAIM.** Brazil’s Federal Police said at 09:16 on 15 September 2026 that, with the federal comptroller (CGU), it launched **Operation Sonar** in Belém.

According to the bulletin:

- 12 search warrants from the 4th Federal Criminal Court in Pará;
- one suspect arrested **in the act** for money laundering (concealment) after officers found **more than R$1.2 million in cash** with no shown lawful source, PF says;
- 12 public servants suspended;
- a **R$17 million** asset freeze;
- bank and tax secrecy lifted for 35 people;
- contracts tied to suspected firms suspended.

PF says it is examining, **in theory**, criminal organization, embezzlement, concussão (extortion by official), passive corruption, bidding crimes, false statements, and laundering.

**FACT.** The bulletin exists and G1 independently reported the same operational outline. **NOT FACT.** Guilt.

The gov.br notice names “a federal public company based in Belém” and **does not name** the firm. Some local outlets named Companhia Docas do Pará; this desk treats that identification as **unconfirmed** against the official text we read.

No public named defendant for a right-of-reply letter. Field is `none` (no public addressee), **not** “pending.” No letter was sent.
