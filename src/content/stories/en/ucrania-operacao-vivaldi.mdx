**OFFICIAL CLAIM (Ukraine).** On 15 September 2026, Brigadier General Andrii Biletskyi, commander of Ukraine’s 3rd Army Corps, said his forces had launched **Operation Vivaldi** in northern Donetsk, against the Russian salient northwest of Lyman.

Reuters reported his figures: 75 square kilometers cleared of infiltration groups and 10 more recaptured. The Institute for the Study of War the same day treats the combined **85 km²** as the first **explicit** Ukrainian confirmation of the counteroffensive, and says Russian sources are beginning to acknowledge deterioration, including geolocated shelling at Kolodyazi.

**ANALYSIS, not our own verification.** This desk did not geolocate the footage. The 85 km² figure remains a **combat claim** until more independent visual confirmation.

**OFFICIAL CLAIM (Ukraine).** ISW cites the Ukrainian Air Force: 200 Russian drones overnight 14–15 September; 187 downed; strikes in seven locations. Kyiv Mayor Vitaliy Klitschko said two petrol stations were hit and 12 civilians injured. Those are one side’s numbers.

Zelensky, ISW notes, restated willingness to a mutual ceasefire on energy targets amid U.S. President Donald Trump’s push. The Kremlin continues to state maximalist aims — another official claim.
