**FACT (agency document).** On 4 September 2026, U.S. Citizenship and Immigration Services posted an alert about a 28 August 2026 order from the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California in **Medani et al. v. Trump et al., 26-cv-6332**.

USCIS says the court:

- **temporarily vacated** policy memo **PM-602-0193** pending further litigation;
- ordered the agency, “to the extent practicable and in good faith,” to resume ordinary adjudication of the plaintiffs’ pending adjustment applications **without applying** that memo for the rest of the Diversity Visa fiscal year;
- certified in part a subclass: FY-2026 DV selectees and derivatives subject to the USCIS DV Hold in PM-602-0193.

USCIS writes that it **strongly disagrees** with the order but **will follow** its terms pending possible further review.

**What this is not.** Not an amnesty. Not a visa guarantee. Not the end of the case. It does not, by itself, dispose of other 2026 challenges to USCIS holds in Rhode Island and San Francisco.

The agency’s position is already on the page. We sent no letter. Field is `not-applicable`, not “pending.”
