This page is the lead story of a **DEMO** newsroom. It exists so the homepage, claim labels, and source cards can be tested in a browser.

ToTo Global Desk is designed for visual evidence journalism: documents, timelines, maps later, source links, case dossiers, and corrections. It is not designed to launder social-media rumor into a headline.

Until reporters replace this file, treat every proper name in the demo case file as fictional and labeled.

## What you can trust on this build

- The **interface** — desks, language switch, dark/light, evidence log — is real software.
- The **editorial rules** on the methodology page are the intended standard.
- The **articles** are placeholders.

## What you cannot treat as news

Any implication that a real contractor, official, or company failed a public contract. That question is only exercised in the DEMO case file, with invented names.
