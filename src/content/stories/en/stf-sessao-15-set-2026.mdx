**What the court document shows.** The [STF public calendar](https://noticias.stf.jus.br/events/mes/) lists, on 15 September 2026, “Sessão Extraordinária – STF” (morning and afternoon) and “Sessão do Pleno.” **That calendar does not name** Alexandre de Moraes, André Mendonça, Daniel Vorcaro, or an inquiry. It does not prove what was voted.

**What the press reports.** [BBC News Brasil](https://www.bbc.com/portuguese/articles/cm0re47gwy21o), [G1](https://g1.globo.com/politica/noticia/2026/09/15/na-retomada-de-julgamento-fachin-vota-para-rejeitar-pedido-para-adiar-julgamento-de-moraes-e-de-analisar-caso-com-o-de-mendonca.ghtml), [UOL](https://noticias.uol.com.br/ao-vivo/2026/09/15/stf-julgamento-sobre-alexandre-de-moraes.htm) and [Folha](https://www1.folha.uol.com.br/poder/2026/09/fachin-suspende-sessao-no-stf-com-ministros-rachados-e-sem-decidir-sobre-analise-de-casos-moraes-e-mendonca.shtml) describe an extraordinary sitting that day on **whether to open** an inquiry involving Justice Moraes. They report the court **ended without deciding** that question.

This desk **has not read** a minute, judgment, or official result note. The outcome is **strongly supported** by converging coverage — not **verified** on a court paper.

## Procedure, not guilt

G1, UOL and Folha say argument stuck on whether to join or split the Moraes and Mendonça files. They describe a **provisional 4–3** tally to keep the analyses **separate**, and a **vista** request by Flávio Dino that interrupted the procedural vote.

UOL’s live blog says Justices Kassio Nunes Marques and Dias Toffoli recused. BBC and Folha, in the passages read here, **do not repeat** that. We log it as **reported / unconfirmed**.

None of this is a conviction. None of it is an order to investigate.

## What this desk has not seen

The Federal Police report, the messages, the Banco Master file. Newsrooms narrate the crisis’s origin; we do not treat those papers’ contents as fact.

BBC attributes to Mendonça a denial of electoral motive. That is reported speech, not a letter to this newsroom.
