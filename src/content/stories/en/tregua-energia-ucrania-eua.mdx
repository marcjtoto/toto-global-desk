**ISW (15 Sept 2026).** The Institute for the Study of War recorded that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky **restated** willingness to a **mutual** ceasefire on energy targets amid U.S. President Donald Trump’s push. ISW says Zelensky tied Ukraine’s participation to U.S. partners ensuring that “Russia is truly ready to stop the war.” ISW also recorded that Dmitry Peskov called the U.S. proposal “a very good idea” while linking any market relief to sanctions.

**AP (Poland-border dispatch).** The Associated Press story at [https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-poland-nato-drones-dbba56f09562f337aa731f7d4bbd1235](https://apnews.com/article/russia-ukraine-war-poland-nato-drones-dbba56f09562f337aa731f7d4bbd1235) is mainly about Russian drones near Poland. In that same piece, AP records that Trump asked Zelensky to **halt strikes on Russian oil refineries and diesel facilities**, citing record U.S. diesel prices, and that Peskov said any call for Kyiv to stop strikes on “civilian economic infrastructure” **can only be welcomed**. Asked about a train strike near the border, Peskov said Russian forces operate across the entire territory of Ukraine and will continue.

No energy-truce agreement was found on the [White House briefings index](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/) reviewed by ToTo Global Desk at 16 Sept 2026, 10:50 America/Chicago. Items visible in that reading include, among others, the first lady’s Ashe County visit (15 Sept) and the Fort McHenry presidential message (13 Sept).

Right of reply: the three parties spoke in public, according to ISW and AP. No letter from this desk. Field is `not-applicable`, not “pending.”
