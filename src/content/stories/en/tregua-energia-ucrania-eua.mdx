**FACT (AP reporting).** On 14 September 2026 the Associated Press reported that Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelensky said Ukraine is ready to **halt strikes on Russian territory** if allies can secure a **genuine** Russian commitment not to hit Ukraine’s critical infrastructure.

The same story records:

- U.S. President Donald Trump, the previous Sunday, called on Zelensky to **stop strikes on Russian oil refineries and diesel facilities**, citing a shortage and record U.S. diesel prices;
- Kremlin spokesman Dmitry Peskov said any call for Kyiv to stop strikes on “civilian economic infrastructure” **can only be welcomed**;
- asked about a train strike near the Poland–Ukraine border, Peskov said Russian forces operate “across the entire territory of Ukraine” and will continue.

**FACT.** None of these sources publish a signed ceasefire.

The [White House briefings page](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/), on this reading, does **not** post an energy-truce agreement. ISW’s 15 Sept assessment also records Zelensky restating willingness to a mutual ceasefire on energy targets — an official claim, not a treaty.

All three parties have spoken in public. No letter from this desk. Field is `not-applicable`, not “pending.” The [White House briefings page](https://www.whitehouse.gov/briefings-statements/), on this reading, does **not** post an energy-truce agreement.
